# Beforest 10% Club — Product Knowledge

## What It Is
The 10% Lifestyle membership by Beforest. 30 person-nights per year for 10 years across all Beforest Collectives. Access, not ownership. A recurring reset practice, not a leisure plan.

## Key Numbers
- ₹17.6 lakhs (introductory) for 10 years of access
- 30 person-nights per year across the decade — 300 nights of intentional living
- 6 collectives currently operating
- 1,300 acres restored from degraded farmland
- 250 families already members
- 7 years of proven impact

## Pricing Framing — CRITICAL
- NEVER break down the price into per-night or per-day costs
- The offer is framed as: 30 nights per year for 10 years — 300 nights of intentional living
- The framing is about rhythm and practice, not cost efficiency
- "Less than a business-class flight" references are acceptable as comparisons, but never as cost-per-day equivalents
- This is a premium brand. Do not use words like: deal, value, budget, cheap, affordable, getaway, vacation, holiday, escape

## What It Is NOT
- NOT real estate — you don't own land. You own access.
- NOT a property investment — no maintenance, no management, no headaches
- NOT a resort membership — this IS the collective membership itself
- NOT a timeshare — the similarity is only at the level of transaction. The difference lies in density, intent, and returning to the land as a way of life rather than as a leisure purchase.
- NOT carry-forward — the aim is rhythm, not accumulation

## What It IS
- Access to a way of life that already exists and is already working
- A recurring reset practice — closer to a discipline than a plan you buy into and forget
- Nature as calibration, not luxury
- A community of 250 families who've already made this choice
- Regeneration, not development — Beforest restores land and creates landscapes that inspire

## Structure Details
- Immediate family can participate
- Children under 12 do not count toward person-nights
- Nights cannot be carried forward — rhythm, not accumulation
- 2:1 weekday-to-weekend balance to keep access workable for everyone
- Subject to availability
