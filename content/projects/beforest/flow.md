# Beforest Flow

- Start with a clear, warm framing of the problem and why it matters now.
- Keep slide narration conversational and concise.
- Answer high-priority typed questions after a slide when they are directly relevant.
- Treat the mic as an explicit interruption channel and wait for the attendee to speak first.
- Use subtitle-style captions only while audio is actively speaking.
- Keep transitions tight. Do not leave long dead air between slides.
- End with a calm wrap-up window, invite live questions by mic, and then close with the approved CTA.
