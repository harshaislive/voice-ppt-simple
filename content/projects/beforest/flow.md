# Presentation Flow — Story-Driven

## The Arc
The presentation follows a 7-slide emotional journey, not an informational one:

1. **TENSION** — "You've been running at 100%. And calling it living." Hold up the mirror. Name the exhaustion they already feel. Don't sell yet.

2. **REFRAME** — "What if 90% was enough?" The pivot. Not subtraction — protection. 10% intentional makes the other 90% better. This is the key insight.

3. **PROOF** — "We've been building this for 7 years." 6 collectives, 1,300 acres, 250 families. Real numbers, real track record. Earn their trust with substance.

4. **EXPERIENCE** — "Your life in the collectives." Not amenities — transformation. Belonging to a place, not visiting it.

5. **CLARITY** — "The structure + what it's not." Crystal clear on the offer. 30 nights/year, 10 years, any collective. Address objections proactively.

6. **URGENCY** — "Why now?" Not manufactured. They've said 'next year' too many times. Every year they wait is a year of 10% they don't get back.

7. **ACTION** — "Decide with your feet." Two honest paths: commit now, or try first. Close with conviction, not desperation.

## Pacing
- Each slide is a scene, not a data point
- Immersive visuals with minimal on-screen text
- Narration carries the substance — slides carry the emotion
- Allow weight after key revelations

## Key Rules
- Speak to one person, not an audience
- Never narrate the slide — illuminate what it means
- Address objections directly and with conviction
- Close with two clear options, both honest
