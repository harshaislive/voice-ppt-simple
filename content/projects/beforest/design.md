# Beforest Design

- Image-first immersive layout.
- Heading should sit below the main image area, not on top of it.
- Supporting text should be capped at 2 lines.
- Subtitles should sit at the bottom of the image stage.
- Mobile layouts must preserve one dominant image surface and avoid dense header rows.
- Keep the mic accessible and isolated from the main visual stage.
- Keep the wrap-up experience simple, mobile-friendly, and one prompt at a time.
