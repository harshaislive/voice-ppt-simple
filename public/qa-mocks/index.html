Field Notes
Directions

Five Q&A interface mocks. Each one keeps the earthy Beforest mood, but pushes the interaction style differently so you can choose what kind of conversation experience you want the app to project.

Mock 1

Editorial Ledger

Tight, premium, newspaper-meets-concierge. Structured message cards and a more serious information design feel.

Calm / Precise / Premium
 Mock 2

Field Notebook

More intimate and human. Looks like a personal notebook with annotations, highlights, and softer conversational rhythm.

Warm / Personal / Reflective
 Mock 3

Quiet Concierge

Sharper and more controlled. Feels like a high-touch guided sales desk rather than a generic chat drawer.

Guided / Confident / Elegant
 Mock 4

Conversation Timeline

Uses temporal flow and milestones. Better if you want the Q&A to feel like an ongoing journey, not isolated bubbles.

Narrative / Progressive / Memorable
 Mock 5

Atelier Panel

The boldest direction. More designed, more branded, and less “chat app” than the others.

Distinct / Branded / Bold
