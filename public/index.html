How it works

Welcome to your private briefing. Here is how to engage with the experience.

Live Briefing

The presentation flows automatically. The AI narrates each slide while text streams in real-time.

Pause & Resume

Need a moment? Use the control button at the bottom-right of the text area to pause the narration.

Slide History

Tap the clock icon in the top bar to see all slides. You can click any finished slide to replay it instantly.

Ask Questions

Type questions anytime using the message icon. The AI will prepare answers to show you at the end.

React Live
